📋 Adoption Information & Guidelines

Before bringing a new best friend home, please review our simple care requirements:

1. Safe & Loving Home

Adopters must provide a clean, secure indoor space protected from extreme weather and busy roads.

2. Age & Family Consent

Primary adopters must be 18+ years old, and everyone in the household must agree to welcoming a pet.

3. Nutrition & Vet Care

Commitment to daily nutritious meals, fresh water, annual vaccinations, and routine veterinary check-ups.

4. No Abandonment Policy

Pets are a lifetime commitment. If circumstances ever change, you must contact us so we can safely rehome them.
